# GATES-ACT v1 to v2 erratum

## Preserved v1 result

The six-replicate OrganoID replay remains frozen in `artifacts/act/`. Four test replicates stopped at
the five-cell initial design and two abstained. The computations are retained for audit.

## Withdrawn interpretation

Because no successful v1 stop consumed a policy-selected post-seed measurement, v1 does not
demonstrate that active acquisition improved a decision. Its 64.16% grid-cell reduction is also not
an actual laboratory-cost estimate because the replay ignored chronology and batch acquisition.

## Replacement primary evidence

Version 2 uses the EPA cortical-neuron MEA dataset and is stored separately in `artifacts/act_v2/`.
It enforces chronological DIV acquisition, compound-group train/calibration/test separation,
biological-effect accounting, plate-session cost, persistence gating, and abstention. The primary
result is five safe post-seed stops and a 5.36% mean reduction in recording sessions.

No original GATES or v1 GATES-ACT file is overwritten by the v2 benchmark.
